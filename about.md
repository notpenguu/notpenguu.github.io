# About

Hi, I'm notpenguu. I'm currently studying for the OSCP exam as well as pursuing a bachelor's of science in data science concurrent with a bachelor's of science in mathematics at my university and my dream is to be a vulnerability researcher or exploit developer. Ever since I was young I was fascinated by computers but I fell in love with the field of offensive cybersecurity in college. In my journey of learning offensive cybersecurity I've come across a lot of articles that left a lot to be desired. I want to make informative articles that stand out from the conventionally shallow offensive cybersecurity article as well as the more daunting academic papers to meaningfully educate people about the field I'm most passionate about which is offensive cybersecurity and specifically binary exploitation.
